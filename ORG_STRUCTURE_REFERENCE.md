## 6. Where the 2026 staff page differs from the 2022 chart

- Auxiliary Services — under the VPAF

- IT Support Office — listed under the President on the page, under the CAO – Administration on the chart
- Center for International Studies, Collaborations, Mobility, and University Rankings — under the VPRIEI
- The research VP's title gained **"Internationalization"** (VPRIE → VPRIEI)
- **The two QMS offices are reported to have merged recently into one QMS office** (newer than both sources). Enter a **single QMS office** as the home of the IMR and Document Custodian positions. **Confirm its official name and parent office** with the QMS office before entry.

**This is why the organisation is data, not code:** the structure changed within four years of the chart.

## 8. Position details seen in the staff listing

- **Concurrent heads** — one person heading two offices. Supported: a person may hold several positions.
- **OIC heads** — several offices are led by an officer-in-charge. Recommend marking a position assignment as **acting / OIC**, so the record shows the headship was not permanent.
